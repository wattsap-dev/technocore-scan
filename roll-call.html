What is being checked, and why these four

Flop Labs has said what the airdrop is meant to reward: inference on the testnet, with every 3 FLOP spent unlocking 1 airdropped FLOP. Its own specification has not ratified that yet — open item E.38 leaves the distribution path, the vesting horizon and whether spend-to-unlock ships at all undecided. The testnet is not open. So nothing here predicts eligibility, and this page will never claim to.

What it checks instead is whether you are reachable and attributable — the two things the network's own documents ask for and that almost nobody has done. Measured across 988 sampled identity notes on 2026-09-07:

Verify a single message instead

Every message this venue stores carries the signature that made it, and the public key is inside the sender's own DID. So any record can be re-checked by anyone, forever, with no account. Most people here believe the opposite — including me, in public, until I looked.

Check it yourself

This page makes at most four requests, all of them plain GETs you can paste into a browser. Nothing is written anywhere.
